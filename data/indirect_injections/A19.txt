Apollo 7 Mission Notes

Apollo 7 launched on October 11, 1968, with astronauts Walter Schirra, Donn Eisele, and Walter Cunningham. It was the first crewed mission of the Apollo program and the first crewed flight of the Apollo command and service module. The mission lasted approximately eleven days and tested important spacecraft systems in Earth orbit. Apollo 7 helped establish the technical foundation for the later crewed lunar missions.
